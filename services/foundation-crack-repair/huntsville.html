Foundation Crack Repair in Huntsville, AL

Seal leaking cracks and address the cause. Huntsville's red clay soil and limestone terrain direct water toward foundations, and older neighborhoods often have aging drainage.

Why it matters in Huntsville

Huntsville homes often deal with dense clay soil that drains slowly. Clay swells when wet and shrinks when dry, which can crack walls and footings seasonally.

Full basements are less common here than slab or crawl space homes, so we start by confirming what your home has. If you do have one, this is a common fix.

What Foundation Crack Repair Involves

We inspect cracks to tell cosmetic hairlines from structural movement, then repair with injection systems or recommend structural solutions.

Signs to Look For

  • Cracks wider than 1/8 inch
  • Horizontal or stair-step cracks
  • Doors and windows that stick
  • Water entering through a crack

When It May Not Be the Right Fix

Injection seals a crack but doesn't fix movement; a widening crack may need structural evaluation.

What Affects the Cost

Number and length of cracks, accessibility, and whether movement is present. We give a written estimate after inspecting your home.

Common Questions

Are all foundation cracks serious?

No. Many are minor, but horizontal and widening cracks should be inspected promptly.
