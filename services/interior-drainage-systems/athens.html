Interior Drainage Systems in Athens, AL

French drains that capture water at the footing and route it to a sump pump. Athens homes of all ages see water issues from clay soils, drainage problems and heavy seasonal rain.

Why it matters in Athens

Athens homes often deal with dense clay soil that drains slowly. Clay traps water against walls, building pressure that shows up as seepage at the floor edge.

Full basements are less common here than slab or crawl space homes, so we start by confirming what your home has. If you do have one, this is a common fix.

What Interior Drainage Systems Involves

We install drain channels beneath the basement slab edge that collect hydrostatic water before it reaches your living space.

Signs to Look For

  • Water seeping where floor meets wall
  • Puddles after heavy rain
  • White mineral deposits (efflorescence)
  • Damp or peeling basement walls

When It May Not Be the Right Fix

If the leak is a single crack, a targeted crack repair may be enough.

What Affects the Cost

Length of wall covered, floor type, and pump needs. We give a written estimate after inspecting your home.
