Exterior Waterproofing in Decatur, AL

Excavate, seal and drain the outside of your foundation wall. Close to the Tennessee River, Decatur properties can deal with a high water table and persistent moisture.

Why it matters in Decatur

Decatur homes often deal with low-lying ground near the Tennessee River. Digging near high groundwater can be difficult, so interior options are sometimes the better route.

Full basements are less common here than slab or crawl space homes, so we start by confirming what your home has. If you do have one, this is a common fix.

What Exterior Waterproofing Involves

Exterior waterproofing applies membrane and drainage to the outside of the foundation, stopping water before it contacts the wall. It is the most thorough fix when access allows.

Signs to Look For

  • Water entering through the wall itself
  • Recurring leaks after interior fixes
  • Downspouts and grading problems
  • Deteriorated old coating

When It May Not Be the Right Fix

It is disruptive and costly; if grading, gutters or an interior system can solve the problem, we'll tell you.

What Affects the Cost

Excavation depth, wall length, landscaping and access. We give a written estimate after inspecting your home.

Common Questions

Is exterior waterproofing worth the cost?

It is invasive but can be the best long-term fix for severe wall leaks.
